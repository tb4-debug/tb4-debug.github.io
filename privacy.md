# Privacy policy for Zahri Briefing

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Zahri Briefing is a private app used only by its owner, Taikhum Bharmal. It has no other users.

## What data it accesses

With the owner's own Google sign-in, the app reads, read-only: the owner's Google Classroom courses, posts, assignments and deadlines; the owner's Google Calendar events; and the Google Drive files attached to the owner's Classroom posts. It never creates, changes or deletes anything in the owner's Google account.

## How the data is used

The data is used only to build the owner's private daily briefing PDF. Summaries are generated with the Google Gemini API and the PDF is sent only to the owner's own Telegram chat.

## Storage and sharing

Access tokens and short text excerpts are stored only on the owner's own home server. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

## How to revoke access

The owner can remove the app's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions and can delete the stored tokens from the server.
