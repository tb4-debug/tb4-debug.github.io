# Zahri Briefing

Zahri Briefing is a private, personal morning-briefing app used only by its owner, Taikhum Bharmal.

Each morning it builds a PDF briefing on the owner's own home server and sends it to the owner on Telegram. With the owner's permission it reads, read-only, the owner's own Google Classroom posts and deadlines, Google Calendar events and the Google Drive files attached to Classroom posts. It also reads public news and market data.

The app is not offered to the public and has no other users.
